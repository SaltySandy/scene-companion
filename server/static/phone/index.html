Scene Companion

Phone web app. Camera and mic go to the laptop. The laptop talks back.

Stopped

On a phone you need HTTPS (Tailscale serve/funnel or a Cloudflare tunnel). Desktop testing can use http://127.0.0.1:8765/phone.

On by default. After Start, say what's in front of me. Turn Listen off to stop sending your voice. While the phone is speaking, the mic is muted.

Describes what the camera sees (Gemini), plus web facts for products, signs and labels (Nimble). Or leave Listen on and say what's in front of me.

Transcript

Rear camera at about 640×480, 8 frames per second, JPEG quality 70 — same as the Android app. Frames are dropped, never queued.
